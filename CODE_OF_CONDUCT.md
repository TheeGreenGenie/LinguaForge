# Code of Conduct

LinguaForge adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be welcoming to people of every background, language and level, including beginners who make lots of mistakes.
- Give and accept corrections kindly. Explain *why* something is wrong, and assume good faith.
- Respect dialects and regional usage. "Not standard" is not the same as "wrong". Explain the difference rather than dismissing it.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or personal or political attacks.
- No publishing other people's private information.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately to the maintainers. Use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
